NYDUCATI HEIC conversion uses heic-to 1.6.6, with libheif 1.23.6 and its HEVC decoder. The decoder is loaded only when native HEIC decoding is unavailable. These components are distributed under their upstream licenses, including LGPL-3.0 for heic-to and libheif. No modifications were made to the supplied decoder bundle.
